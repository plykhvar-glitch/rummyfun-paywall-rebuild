What this is

We played Rummy Fun as a new player, then rebuilt the one screen where coins are sold.

Adapty powers in-app purchases for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market, not inside one game. We installed Rummy Fun, played through Marianne’s tutorial, opened the Shop and read your App Store and Google Play listings.

Your Shop is your own screen, drawn by your game. It stays that way. Adapty does not replace it. It tells it what to show, to whom, and which version wins:

Remote Config

A JSON payload on an Adapty paywall: which packs, in which order, which badges, where the Shop opens. Your client reads it and draws it.

Segments

New players, payers and high spenders each get their own payload, built from purchase history Adapty already holds.

A/B tests

Two payloads, one audience, revenue per player as the winner. No build, no store review, on iOS and Android at once.

We did not redesign your Shop

The wood header, the autumn garland, the cream-framed teal cards, the green bonus bars, Popular and Best deal, the orange Starter Pack with its timer and the VIP line all stay. Only what the Shop is told to show changes.
